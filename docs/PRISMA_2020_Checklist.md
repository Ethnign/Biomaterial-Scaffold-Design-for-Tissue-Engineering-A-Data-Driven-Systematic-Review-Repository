## Notes for the authors

1. **Items 24a/24b, previously the clearest gap, are now resolved**: Section 2.1 (Protocol and Reporting Standard) explicitly states that this review was not prospectively registered and that no protocol was published in advance, satisfying PRISMA 2020's requirement for an explicit statement either way.
2. **Item 13d, previously an open item, is now resolved**: Section 2.1 explicitly states that a narrative, structured synthesis was adopted instead of a meta-analysis, following the Synthesis without Meta-analysis (SWiM) reporting guideline, and explains why (insufficiently uniform outcomes/assays/time points for a common effect metric). Items 12, 14, 15, and 20b/22 remain correctly marked "not applicable" on the basis that no meta-analysis or formal certainty-of-evidence framework was attempted.
3. **Items 13e/13f/20c/20d (heterogeneity, sensitivity analyses)** are open items consistent with what the independent review report flagged (in particular, no sensitivity analysis on the 37 unretrieved full texts). These remain honestly unresolved in this checklist rather than papered over.
4. **Item 2 (Abstract)** — PRISMA has a separate 12-item "PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts" checklist; this document does not reproduce it separately. If the target journal requires a structured abstract, that checklist should be completed against the final published abstract format.
5. **Section-number cross-references in this table were fully re-verified against the manuscript's current numbering on 2026-10-19** (items 6, 7, 8, 16a, 16b, and 23c had drifted to stale section/table numbers after earlier reorganization and were corrected here); re-verify again if sections, tables, or figures are reordered in a later revision, since none of these references are auto-updating in this standalone Markdown file.
