## What can be reproduced from the files in this repository, and what cannot

- **Fully reproducible from public files:** the five content-axis networks and their centrality statistics, starting from `notebooks/pipeline_Q3_BioBERT_5ejes_CORREGIDO.ipynb` — *provided* the same PDF corpus is supplied (see `data/raw/` and the note below). Fixed seed = 33.
- **Not reproducible from an automated script:** Supplementary Tables S1–S5, the PRISMA diagram, and the risk-of-bias assessment are all the product of manual expert reading/judgement against the 78 studies' full texts, not a deterministic pipeline. Independent replication of these requires repeating the manual assessment (the point of the second-assessor check and the planned multi-rater Cohen's kappa described in `docs/RoB_Estado_y_Pendientes.md`), not re-running code.
- **Not currently included:** the 78 studies' full-text PDFs themselves (copyright), and the raw Scopus/Web of Science export files (see `data/raw/README.md`).
