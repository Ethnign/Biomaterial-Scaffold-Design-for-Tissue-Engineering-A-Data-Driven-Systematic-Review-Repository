# results/networks/

The five complete, unfiltered entity co-occurrence networks produced by `notebooks/pipeline_Q3_BioBERT_5ejes_CORREGIDO.ipynb` (one per content axis), in Gephi's `.gexf` format. Degree- and betweenness-centrality statistics reported in the manuscript (Table `tab:q3axes`, Section 4 text) are computed on these complete networks, not on the degree-filtered subgraphs shown in the rendered figures.
